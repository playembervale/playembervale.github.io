New in this build

Fresh from the forge

Everything below is in the downloads. Every picture is drawn by the game itself.

Progression

The Hunter's Bestiary

A new tab in your Journal: one page per boss and miniboss, in the exact order you must hunt them. Health, damage, where it lives, how to find or call it, how it fights, what it drops, and a DEFEATED stamp. Each lure only works once the hunt before it is done, so there's always one clear next step.

Bosses

Six biome minibosses

The Bog Hag hides in the swamp fog, the Dune Wyrm bursts up out of the sand under you, the Hive Queen calls her hornets, the Cave Warden is a rock troll that rolls at you as a boulder, and the Frost Matron rains ice in the tundra. Each has its own lure, attacks and loot.

Loot

Every boss has a signature weapon

No more praying for a lucky drop. Every boss always drops its trophy, and the trophy is crafted into that boss's own weapon: the Bogrot Dagger, Sandfang Spear, Queen's Sting, Boulderfist Hammer, Frostbite Glaive, Broadside Blunderbuss, Gullbreaker Hand Cannon and more. Power climbs with the hunt; the Seraph Blade stays the strongest.

Performance

Smooth on any phone

One-tap Low / Medium / High graphics, plus a new Fast resolution that draws the game at 720p and scales it up, pixel-perfect. On a high-resolution phone that is less than half the work per frame. New installs on phones start on Fast.

Controls

Cleaner touch controls

Redesigned on-screen controls: frosted-glass buttons with crisp icons, a joystick that glows toward where you push, and your potion count in a badge on the heal button. Ground spikes now burst up out of the earth with a crack of warning first, instead of flying.

Hidden boss

The tree that isn't a tree

Ancient banyans stand in the deep forests like any other tree. Take an axe to one and it wakes: roots burst up under you, its crown sheds razor leaves, and get too close and the ground throws you clear. Only an axe can cut it. Its heartwood makes the Treant Sword, living wood that grows stronger with every hunt.

Bosses

Boss fights in three stages

At two thirds and one third of its health every boss stops the fight and talks to you, each in its own voice: the Colossus grunts, the Seraph speaks like a god, Brassbeard is all pirate. Then it comes back faster: 1.25x, then 1.5x.

Music

A soundtrack that hits harder as you go

Every boss has its own theme, and an Overdrive remix that kicks in at stage 2: faster, a half-step higher, harder drums. The last stage drops straight into the climax. Background and battle music now have separate volume sliders.

Final boss

The Ember Seraph, reborn

The final boss is no longer a statue with wings. Six wings beat in three pairs, its eyes blink and follow you, two rings of eyes turn around its burning core, and every stage it wakes up a little more.

Defenders

Villages fight back

Every village has a Stone Guardian golem, spear guards and an archer on a stone lookout tower. They hunt down monsters at night, and fallen defenders return after a while.

Villagers

Nights are dangerous, for everyone

Monsters attack villagers too. At nightfall everyone walks home and stays inside. Anyone struck down is back home by morning. You only talk to people face to face: both outside, or both in the same house.

Combat

Weapons move like the real thing

Every weapon sits in the fist at its grip. Hammers come down over the head, spears thrust, thrown weapons leave the hand at the release, long guns are held with both hands and kick back with a muzzle flash.

Arsenal

20 new weapons

Boomerangs and chakrams that come back to your hand, flails you whirl on a chain, whips that crack at the tip, crossbows, a flamethrower, bombs, summoning staffs, spell tomes, a reaper's scythe and a duelist's rapier.

Inventory

An off hand

Keep a torch, food, a potion or ammo in your other hand. A torch lights your way while you fight, and quick-eat takes off-hand food first. Swords and tools stay in the main hand.

Crafting

Look before you craft

Tap any recipe to see a full card: damage, speed, knockback, reach, mana, ammo, effects, what you have of each ingredient and which station you need. Only the Craft button crafts.

Getting around

Stairs, platforms and ladders

Houses have stairs between floors, you can drop through platforms, and pressing down beside a ladder hole puts you on it. Grab and let go of any ladder with R or the GRAB button.

Ocean

The drowned and the deep

Skeleton corsairs in torn coats walk the sea floor. Swim, watch your breath, and stay clear of whales that breach and come crashing down on boats.

Every land has its own sound

Songbirds and woodpeckers in the forest by day, owls and wolves at night, cicadas on the savanna, frogs in the wetland, wind in the mountains, surf on the coast, rushing rivers and waterfalls, and the muffled hush underwater. Weather follows each land's real climate and seasons, and the worlds themselves are tidier: villages sit on level ground and trees come down whole.
